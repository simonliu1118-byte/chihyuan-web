# BD-031 — Sales work-order customer entry stays simple; no automatic customer guessing

**Status:** CONFIRMED

## Business purpose

A CY Web sales work order is a field/pre-ERP work order created by sales staff outside the office. Internal staff later completes the authoritative SMART ERP customer/order setup and then fills the ERP information back into CY Web.

The Web workflow must therefore favor quick capture in the field and must not add automatic customer-matching logic that the business did not ask for.

## Existing-customer entry

Sales staff may select an existing customer through the customer lookup on the sales work-order screen.

When an existing customer is selected:

- the user selects/searches by the visible customer number/customer record;
- CY Web fills the customer name from the Customer master;
- CY Web carries the internal `customer_id` automatically;
- the user does not see or enter the technical `customer_id`;
- when a customer number is present, the customer name is system-derived and must not be freely typed as a conflicting value.

The Customer detail page must also provide an action such as **建立銷貨單**. Starting a work order from that page pre-fills the selected Customer relationship, including its internal `customer_id` and available customer number/name.

## Name-only field entry

Sales staff may also create a work order by typing only a customer name.

In this mode:

- `customer_no` may be empty;
- `customer_id` may be null;
- the typed customer name is retained as the work-order customer-name snapshot/input;
- the work order may be saved in `created / 已建檔` state;
- CY Web does **not** guess, fuzzy-match, auto-link, or silently merge the name-only entry with any Customer master record.

This intentionally keeps the field workflow simple.

## Internal-staff completion

For a name-only work order, internal staff is responsible for checking the Customer module and determining whether:

- an existing Customer already represents the customer but still lacks an ERP customer number; or
- a new Customer record must be created after the formal customer is established in SMART ERP.

That reconciliation is a deliberate human operation, not an automatic matching algorithm.

After the Customer master has the correct ERP customer number, internal staff fills the customer number into the work order. CY Web performs an exact Customer-master lookup by that customer number and then automatically supplies:

- the correct `customer_id`;
- the Customer-master name shown on the work order.

The customer name is no longer free-entry once the work order has a formal customer-number relationship.

Internal staff then fills the SMART ERP sales-order number. The existing Order lifecycle rule remains: the first ERP-order-number fill establishes the `issued / 已出單` stage.

## Data-integrity rule

The permitted customer states on a work order are therefore conceptually:

```text
A. linked existing customer
   customer_id   = present
   customer_no   = present when the Customer is ERP-qualified
   customer name = derived from Customer master / retained document snapshot as appropriate

B. field name only
   customer_id   = null
   customer_no   = null
   customer name = required free text
```

The application must not persist a user-invented combination of a formal customer number and an unrelated free-typed customer name.

## Relation to BD-001 and BD-024

This decision complements, rather than replaces:

- BD-001: Customer identity may exist before an ERP customer number exists;
- BD-024: CY Web owns the field/pre-ERP work-order and fulfillment workflow while SMART ERP owns the authoritative formal sales order.

It deliberately rejects an earlier over-complicated proposal to automatically infer or suggest Customer identity from a name-only work order.

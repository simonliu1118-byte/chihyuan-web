# BD-031 — Sales work-order customer entry stays simple; no automatic customer guessing

**Status:** CONFIRMED / CLARIFIED

## Business purpose

A CY Web sales work order is a field/pre-ERP work order created by sales staff outside the office. Internal staff later completes or verifies the authoritative SMART ERP customer/order setup and then fills the ERP information back into CY Web.

The Web workflow must therefore favor quick capture in the field and must not add automatic customer-matching logic that the business did not ask for.

## Customer identity outside the Order exception

A CY Web Customer may exist before SMART ERP assigns a formal customer number.

When Customer master data is created, CY Web automatically creates the immutable technical `customer_id`. Users do not type or manage this internal ID.

Therefore Customer-owned records such as Visits use the existing Customer identity even when `customer_no` is still null:

```text
Customer created in CY Web
  -> customer_id exists immediately
  -> customer_no may still be NULL
  -> Visits and other Customer-owned records link by customer_id
  -> later SMART ERP qualification fills customer_no without changing customer_id
```

The name-only/no-`customer_id` exception described below applies specifically to rapid field sales-work-order capture. It is not the normal Customer/Visit identity model.

## Existing-customer entry

Sales staff may select an existing Customer through the customer lookup on the sales-work-order screen.

The selected Customer may be either:

- already ERP-qualified and therefore have `customer_no`; or
- already present in CY Web from prior prospect/Visit activity but still have `customer_no = NULL`.

When an existing Customer is selected:

- CY Web carries the internal `customer_id` automatically;
- the user does not see or enter the technical `customer_id`;
- CY Web fills the customer display name from the Customer master;
- current `customer_no` is carried when available, but its absence does not prevent selecting the Customer;
- when a formal customer number is present, the user must not persist an unrelated free-typed customer name as if it were the same formal relationship.

The Customer detail page must also provide an action such as **建立銷貨單**. Starting a work order from that page pre-fills the selected Customer relationship, including its internal `customer_id` and available customer number/name.

## Name-only field entry

Sales staff may also create a sales work order by typing only a customer name when they cannot reliably identify the correct Customer record while in the field.

In this Order-specific mode:

- `customer_no` is empty;
- `customer_id` may temporarily be null;
- the typed customer name is retained as the work-order customer-name snapshot/input;
- the work order may be saved in `created / 已建檔` state;
- CY Web does **not** guess, fuzzy-match, auto-link, or silently merge the name-only entry with any Customer master record.

This intentionally keeps field order capture simple without weakening the normal Customer/Visit identity model.

## Internal-staff completion

For a name-only work order, internal staff deliberately reconciles the order to a Customer.

The result may be:

- an existing Customer already represents the customer and may or may not yet have an ERP customer number; or
- a new Customer record must be created, which immediately receives a CY Web `customer_id` even if its ERP customer number is still pending.

Once internal staff explicitly identifies the correct Customer, CY Web fills the work order's `customer_id` from that Customer and uses Customer-master display information as the linked relationship.

If the Customer is not yet ERP-qualified, `customer_no` may remain null until SMART ERP creates the formal customer record. When SMART ERP later assigns the customer number, it is filled back into the Customer master without changing `customer_id`.

Internal staff then fills the SMART ERP sales-order number when the formal ERP sales order has been created. The existing Order lifecycle rule remains: the first ERP-order-number fill establishes the `issued / 已出單` stage.

## Data-integrity rule

The permitted Customer states on a sales work order are conceptually:

```text
A. linked Customer
   customer_id   = present
   customer_no   = present or NULL depending on ERP qualification
   customer name = derived from Customer master / retained document snapshot as appropriate

B. field name only, Order-specific temporary state
   customer_id   = NULL
   customer_no   = NULL
   customer name = required free text
```

The application must not persist a user-invented combination of a formal customer number and an unrelated free-typed customer name.

Customer/Visit records do not use state B: Visits require an existing Customer identity and therefore a `customer_id`.

## Relation to BD-001 and BD-024

This decision complements, rather than replaces:

- BD-001: Customer identity may exist before an ERP customer number exists;
- BD-024: CY Web owns the field/pre-ERP work-order and fulfillment workflow while SMART ERP owns the authoritative formal sales order.

It deliberately rejects automatic inference or fuzzy Customer matching from a name-only sales work order.

## Consolidation note

The Final Data Dictionary must reflect the clarified split:

- Customer/Visit identity exists through `customer_id` even before ERP qualification;
- `sales_work_orders.customer_id` is nullable only for the explicit name-only field-entry state;
- name-only work orders require a customer-name snapshot/input and deliberate internal-staff reconciliation;
- lookup selection may target a Customer whose `customer_no` is still null.

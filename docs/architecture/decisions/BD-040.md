# BD-040 — Cancelling a confirmed outsourcing outbound voids the outsourcing order

**Status:** CONFIRMED

## Decision

For Outsourcing, **取消出庫** is not an intermediate workflow step that returns the same order to `pending_outbound / 待出庫`.

Once a confirmed outbound is cancelled, CY Web performs the required outbound reversal and the outsourcing order enters the terminal state:

```text
voided / 作廢
```

This keeps the business meaning consistent: an outsourcing order that is no longer going to proceed ends as `作廢`, rather than returning to an active waiting state.

## Stock and history behavior

Cancelling outbound must still preserve the physical-event history established by BD-026, BD-027 and BD-032:

- the original confirmed outbound event remains in history;
- the original stock movement is not deleted or rewritten;
- a linked compensating/reversal stock movement restores the contractor-material balance;
- the cancellation/void event records actor and timestamp;
- the outsourcing-order identity and original business facts remain queryable;
- the final order status becomes `voided / 作廢`.

The resulting stock balance is derived from the original movement plus the reversal movement.

## Correction is different from cancellation

If goods really were sent out and the outsourcing work is still valid, but the recorded outbound data is wrong, the user must use **更正出庫** rather than **取消出庫**.

`更正出庫` preserves the active outsourcing workflow and applies controlled before/after correction plus the necessary delta/compensating stock movements.

`取消出庫` means the outbound/business flow is being abandoned and therefore ends the outsourcing order as `voided / 作廢`.

## Downstream states

If receiving, pricing or payment has already occurred, their business effects must first be reversed through the existing controlled reversal chain, or through an equivalent atomic controlled cancellation workflow that produces the same auditable result. The system must not erase downstream facts.

After the outbound itself is finally reversed, the outsourcing order ends in `voided / 作廢`.

## Pending orders

BD-027 remains unchanged for an outsourcing order that has never had a confirmed outbound event:

```text
pending_outbound / 待出庫
```

Such an unreferenced/unexecuted order may still be hard-deleted under the existing authorization and deletion-audit rule. Once a real outbound has ever been confirmed, the outsourcing order must not be hard-deleted; cancellation ends in `voided / 作廢` instead.

## Supersession / clarification

This decision resolves the open cancellation-end-state question in BD-027 and supersedes the part of BD-032 that described **取消出庫** as returning the workflow to a state where the same planned order could be corrected and sent out again.

If a new outsourcing job is needed after a voided order, it is represented as a new active outsourcing order rather than reactivating the voided one.

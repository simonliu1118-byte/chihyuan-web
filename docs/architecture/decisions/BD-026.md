# BD-026 — Outsourcing stock becomes contractor-held only when outbound is confirmed

**Status:** CONFIRMED

## Decision

For outsourcing/material operations, a newly created outsourcing order in `pending_outbound / 待出庫` represents a **planned outbound quantity only**. It must not increase the contractor's actual on-hand material stock.

Contractor-held stock increases only when the outbound operation is explicitly confirmed and the workflow moves to:

```text
pending_outbound / 待出庫
        ↓ confirm outbound
outbound / 已出庫
```

The stock movement and the business transition therefore occur at the same operational event.

## Rationale

The Legacy Desktop implementation updates contractor stock while saving the outsourcing order, before the order has actually been marked `已出庫`. This can make system stock show material at the contractor even though the physical goods are still waiting to leave Chihyuan.

That Legacy timing is treated as an implementation defect/technical shortcut and is **not** part of the CY Web business contract.

The canonical model must distinguish:

- planned/pending outbound quantity;
- confirmed outbound stock movement;
- actual contractor-held balance.

A UI may show projected information such as:

```text
actual contractor stock: 100
pending outbound:          50
projected after outbound: 150
```

but the pending `50` must not be included in authoritative actual stock until outbound is confirmed.

## Stock-ledger implication

The target stock model should create the authoritative supply/outbound movement when `pending_outbound -> outbound` is confirmed, with structured facts sufficient for reconciliation, including at least:

- outsourcing order reference;
- contractor reference;
- part/item reference;
- quantity and unit/base quantity as applicable;
- actor;
- effective time/date;
- movement/event type.

The contractor stock balance is derived/reconcilable from stock movements rather than being authoritative solely because a mutable balance field was changed.

## Reversal / deletion boundary

This decision does **not** yet define how an already-confirmed outbound movement may be reversed, cancelled or hard-deleted. Those are separate controlled operations because they affect real stock history and must be resolved explicitly in the outsourcing workflow review.

## Migration implication

Existing Legacy data must be interpreted from the stored workflow state and related operational records rather than assuming that every historical mutable contractor balance reflects the correct physical timing. Migration/reconciliation should rebuild stock from domain facts and structured adjustments wherever reliable.

## Consolidation note

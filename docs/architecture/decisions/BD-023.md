# BD-023 — Customer frequent items may include unfiled free-text products

**Status:** CONFIRMED

## Decision

CY Web preserves the Legacy ability to record a customer's frequent/commonly used products even when a product does not yet exist as a formal SMART ERP Item master record.

A frequent-item entry may therefore be either:

1. a formal Item relation; or
2. an unfiled free-text customer-information entry.

Canonical shape:

```text
customer_frequent_items.customer_id
customer_frequent_items.item_id          nullable
customer_frequent_items.custom_item_name nullable
customer_frequent_items.note             nullable
customer_frequent_items.sort_order
```

Exactly one product identity path should normally be populated for a row: a formal `item_id`, or a free-text/custom product description.

## Business boundary

An unfiled frequent item is customer intelligence/reference data. It is **not** a formal Item master and does not receive a CY-generated official item number.

This preserves BD-004: formal Items belong to SMART ERP and must first exist there with the official ERP item number.

Free-text frequent items may be used for customer profile, visit preparation, competitive-product notes and product-demand intelligence.

If that product later becomes a formal SMART ERP Item, an authorized user may link/reconcile the frequent-item entry to the resulting `item_id` without creating a duplicate Item master.

## Transaction boundary

The existence of a free-text frequent item does not authorize CY Web to create an ERP sales transaction from it. Formal ERP-owned transactions require the appropriate SMART ERP master data and remain subject to the system-of-record boundaries defined elsewhere.

## Migration implication

Legacy `Frequent.frequentData` entries with a formal item should reconcile to `item_id` where unambiguous. Legacy unfiled/custom entries remain free-text frequent-item rows and must not be discarded or converted into fake Item master records.

# BD-048 — SMART ERP customer number may be corrected or changed without changing customer identity

**Status:** CONFIRMED

## Decision

The SMART ERP customer number stored in CY Web is a business identifier, not the Customer entity's relational identity.

A Customer may still exist before a formal SMART ERP customer number has been assigned. When present, `customers.customer_no` must be unique. Unlike the original immutability statement in BD-001, however, an assigned customer number **may later be changed** when the authoritative SMART ERP number changes or when an authorized correction is required.

The immutable identity remains:

```text
customers.id
```

All Customer relationships continue to reference `customer_id`, never `customer_no` or customer name.

## Change behavior

Changing `customer_no`:

- does not create a new Customer entity;
- does not rewrite relational foreign keys;
- does not change existing Customer history ownership;
- must preserve uniqueness of the current non-null customer number;
- must be handled as an explicit, auditable master-data correction/remapping action;
- should eventually reconcile against SMART ERP through the ERP integration boundary when that integration exists.

## Historical documents

Formal historical documents continue to follow BD-006. If a document froze `customer_no_snapshot` when it became formal, changing the current Customer master number does not silently rewrite that historical snapshot unless a future explicit business migration/correction decision says otherwise.

## Supersession

This decision supersedes only the portions of BD-001 that state an assigned `customers.customer_no` is permanently immutable.

The following BD-001 rules remain active:

- a prospect/customer may exist before a customer number exists;
- the customer number is ERP-owned and not generated by CY Web;
- the customer number is nullable before ERP qualification and unique when present;
- the internal Customer ID is immutable and is the only relationship key.

No automatic old-customer-number alias/search-history requirement is introduced by this decision. That behavior may be added later only if a concrete ERP or business need requires it.

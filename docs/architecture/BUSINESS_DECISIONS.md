# CY Web Business Decisions — BD-001 through BD-018

> Project: Chihyuan Enterprise Management System (CY Web)
>
> This file is an architecture decision summary, not a governance rules source. The detailed pre-consolidation text is preserved at `archive/BUSINESS_DECISIONS_001_018_PRE_CONSOLIDATION.md`.
>
> For the complete decision index and supersession map, read `decisions/README.md` first.

## Global consolidation notes

- BD-047 confirms that the current GAS / Google Sheets dataset is development/test data and is **not migrated** into production D1. Earlier Legacy-migration implications in BD-001 through BD-018 are therefore non-operative.
- BD-048 supersedes only the portion of BD-001 that made an assigned customer number permanently immutable.
- BD-035 supersedes the transaction-gating and mandatory-reopen portions of BD-014.

## BD-001 — Customer internal identity and customer number lifecycle

**Status:** CONFIRMED, with customer-number immutability superseded by BD-048.

- A prospect/customer may exist before a formal SMART ERP customer number exists.
- `customers.id` exists immediately, is immutable, and is the relationship key for all Customer-linked data.
- `customers.customer_no` is nullable before SMART ERP qualification, unique when present, ERP-owned, and never generated by CY Web.
- Visits and other pre-commercial records may exist while `customer_no IS NULL`.
- Quotes/orders and every other relation reference `customer_id`, never customer number/name.
- Per BD-048, an assigned customer number may later be corrected or changed without changing `customers.id`.

## BD-002 — SMART ERP remains the primary ERP; CY Web is the Web/mobile complementary system

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- SMART ERP remains the primary ERP and authority for ERP-owned identifiers/business facts.
- CY Web complements SMART ERP for Web/mobile workflows, field work, cross-module workflows, and extension data.
- CY Web must not duplicate official SMART ERP numbering logic where ERP is authoritative.
- CY Web internal IDs and SMART ERP business identifiers are separate concepts.
- Future ERP integration belongs behind an adapter/synchronization boundary.

## BD-003 — Long-term read-only SMART ERP database integration

**Status:** CONFIRMED LONG-TERM DIRECTION.

- The long-term direction is read-only retrieval of authoritative SMART ERP data through a local ERP Bridge / Sync Agent.
- Do not expose the local SQL database directly to the public Internet.
- Production integration must not use unrestricted administrator credentials.
- Use a dedicated least-privilege read-only database identity, preferably against approved Views where practical.
- The bridge initiates authenticated outbound HTTPS to CY Web.
- CY Web must not write directly to SMART ERP unless a future explicit project changes this boundary.
- Imported ERP fields remain ERP-owned/read-only where appropriate; physical ERP schema details must not leak through all CY Web modules.

## BD-004 — Item master is ERP-owned; item numbers may be remapped

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- A formal Item must already exist in SMART ERP before becoming a formal CY Web Item master record.
- `items.id` is immutable and is the relationship key.
- `items.item_no` is ERP-owned, required for a formal Item, unique as the current number, and may change through controlled ERP-driven remapping.
- Item number must never be a database relationship key.
- Historical item-number behavior follows BD-006 and BD-011.

## BD-005 — Internal IDs are technical, not human-readable business codes

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- Internal primary keys optimize for uniqueness, immutability, relational reliability, and database/index efficiency.
- Human-readable business codes belong in explicit fields such as `customer_no` and `item_no`.
- Internal IDs must not encode mutable year/region/department/salesperson/category/status/ERP-number semantics.
- Normal users should not need to see technical database IDs.
- A simple D1/SQLite integer primary key remains acceptable unless a concrete requirement justifies another physical key type.

## BD-006 — Formal transaction documents freeze business snapshots

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- Master/reference screens normally show current master data through stable foreign keys.
- Draft documents may refresh/reselect current master data while editable.
- When a document becomes formal/issued, fields needed to reproduce that historical document are frozen as explicit snapshots.
- Typical Customer snapshots may include number/name/address/tax/contact values actually used by the document.
- Typical Item-line snapshots may include item number/name/spec/unit plus transaction price/terms.
- Live `customer_id` / `item_id` relationships remain for navigation and identity.
- Later master edits do not silently rewrite frozen historical document content.
- BD-011 defines the explicit exceptional item-renumbering operation.

## BD-007 — Contractor/vendor may be a person or an organization

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- Contractor represents the outsourcing/vendor counterparty and may be an individual, company, factory, workshop/studio, or other organization.
- Contractor identity is a stable `contractor_id`; names are not foreign keys.
- Organizational contractors may have multiple subordinate contacts.
- Renaming a contractor or replacing contacts does not change the underlying entity relationship.
- Historical outsourcing/pricing documents use snapshots where historical reconstruction requires them.

## BD-008 — Customer region is geographic; business ownership is organizational

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- Customer region means Taiwan county/city geographic classification.
- Broad north/central/south groupings are presentation/grouping metadata, not the stored geographic identity.
- Geographic region is separate from responsible department and salesperson.
- The canonical model uses a controlled county/city reference with a stable key/ID.

## BD-009 — Customer region is independent from individual addresses

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- `customers.region_id` is a customer-level geographic classification.
- A Customer may have multiple addresses across different counties/cities.
- Address add/edit/delete must not silently rewrite an already-selected customer region.
- Address parsing may suggest a region when useful, but changing an established region requires explicit user action.

## BD-010 — Configurable business lookups are separate from fixed workflow states

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- Administrator-managed classifications such as Customer categories, Item categories, departments, WorkLog categories/platforms, and descriptive Customer statuses use stable lookup identities and editable display labels.
- Referenced lookup rows are normally disabled rather than hard-deleted.
- Workflow state-machine codes that drive program behavior are fixed application contracts and are not arbitrary Settings rows.
- UI labels may later be customizable without changing stable workflow codes/transition semantics.

## BD-011 — Previous SMART ERP item numbers remain searchable until explicitly retired

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- When SMART ERP changes an official Item number, the previous number remains associated with the same immutable `items.id` as a historical alias by default.
- New transactions use the current Item number.
- Previous numbers remain searchable until the user explicitly retires them.
- A future explicit Full Renumbering operation may validate complete old→new mappings, update authorized item-number snapshots, stop old-code search, and remove old mappings/history when the user requests full retirement.
- That future item-code operation is separate from the abandoned Legacy GAS/Sheet migration in BD-047.

## BD-012 — WorkLog scoring rules are configurable; finalized scores are frozen

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- Authorized administrators may change scoring configuration for future work.
- Draft/editable WorkLogs may preview current rules.
- Once a WorkLog is finalized/reviewed, its historical scoring result is frozen.
- Later scoring-rule changes do not retroactively recalculate finalized WorkLogs.
- Historical reporting uses stored finalized score evidence, not current scoring configuration.
- Corrections/reopening must be explicit controlled actions, not silent recalculation.

## BD-013 — ERP qualification is separate from configurable customer business status

**Status:** CONFIRMED, with closed-business transaction behavior refined by BD-035.

- ERP qualification is derived from the authoritative Customer-number relationship; do not add a second manually maintained `prospect/formal` flag.
- Customer business status is a separate company-managed classification.
- Normal business-status lookup rows may be added/renamed/reordered/disabled as configuration.
- `closed_business / 已歇業` remains a protected semantic/status concept and visible state, but BD-035 determines that it does not by itself block supported CY Web workflows.

## BD-014 — Closed-business customers remain historical but cannot create new commercial transactions

**Status:** PARTIALLY SUPERSEDED by BD-035.

Still active:

- Closed-business Customers remain searchable and historically retained.
- Their status remains visibly distinguishable in Customer UI/history.

Superseded:

- `closed_business` no longer acts as a CY Web application-level transaction gate.
- CY Web no longer requires a mandatory reopen/status-change step before supported workflows.

See BD-035 for current behavior.

## BD-015 — Customer visit person may link to a contact but always keeps a visit-time snapshot

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- A Visit may optionally reference an existing Customer contact.
- The actual person/name used for the Visit is stored as a historical text snapshot.
- An unlisted person may be entered without first creating a Contact master row.
- Later Contact edits/renames/deactivation do not silently rewrite the Visit-time snapshot.

## BD-016 — Customer tax ID may repeat; duplicates require a strong warning

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- `customers.tax_id` is nullable and is **not** globally unique.
- A valid Taiwan tax ID may legitimately appear on more than one Customer record.
- Duplicate entry triggers a strong warning showing matching Customer records, but is not a hard database rejection.
- Tax ID is indexed/searchable but is never a foreign key or substitute for immutable Customer identity.

## BD-017 — Quantities and monetary values support decimal precision

**Status:** CONFIRMED.

- Business quantities, unit prices, costs, subtotals, totals and other applicable values support decimals.
- Storage/calculation precision is separate from display formatting.
- Intermediate calculations must not repeatedly round merely because the UI displays fewer decimals.
- Authoritative money handling must use an exact/fixed-point strategy rather than binary floating-point semantics.
- Exact scale rules are refined by BD-018 and BD-019 and may still require SMART ERP alignment for rounding behavior.

## BD-018 — TWD formal monetary amounts use a two-decimal baseline pending SMART ERP confirmation

**Status:** CONFIRMED BASELINE.

- Formal TWD monetary amounts currently use a two-decimal baseline.
- The design must remain adjustable if verified SMART ERP scale/rounding behavior differs.
- BD-019 separately defines higher precision for quantity and unit-price/cost inputs.
- Line-rounding, tax-rounding, exact rounding method, and final ERP alignment remain implementation/business-verification items before financial calculation logic is frozen.
